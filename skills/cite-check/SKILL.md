---
name: cite-check
description: Check that every in-text citation in artifacts/paper.md appears in artifacts/research.md and that unverified items stay tagged.
---

1. List every in-text citation key or author-year in `artifacts/paper.md`.
2. Match each to a row in `artifacts/research.md`.
3. Flag missing rows, year/title drift, and any `[UNVERIFIED]` source used
   as settled evidence.
4. Hand the list to the auditor. Do not edit the paper.
